\section{Managerial, Policy, and Societal Implications}

Beyond its forecasting function, the cohort-based framework developed in this study operates as a diagnostic instrument that translates biological herd dynamics into decision-driven intelligence for distinct audiences. Because the model resolves each value chain into age and sex-structured cohorts advanced on a quarterly basis, its outputs speak simultaneously to herd and enterprise managers, to national and county policymakers, and to the broader societal objectives of food security, livelihood resilience, and environmental sustainability.

\subsection{Managerial Implications}
At the enterprise level, the framework's principal contribution is diagnostic rather than descriptive: instead of reporting aggregate stock figures, it identifies precisely where within a production system growth is being lost by isolating the cohorts and transition processes that constrain performance---e.g., elevated calf, lamb, and kid mortality, depressed reproductive rates, and sub-optimal off-take structures. This granularity is consequential because, as evidence from comparable East African rangeland systems indicates, the sale and retention rates of breeding and mature females exert the greatest influence on long-run population trajectories.% TODO: cite (tuffa2017, hary2004)

Managers may therefore draw on model outputs to rebalance herd structure, optimise the timing of culling and destocking, and direct animal-health and breeding interventions toward the cohorts yielding the highest marginal returns. The quarterly time step further aligns the framework with the seasonality of operational decision-making. By coupling projected intake requirements to a dynamic, forage-based feed balance, it provides an early-warning capacity through which anticipated dry-season deficits can prompt pre-emptive interventions---fodder conservation, strategic procurement, or timely destocking---before animal condition and market value decline.% TODO: cite (riviere1991, meshesha2019)
Such diagnostics ultimately support the targeted interventions needed to realise the projected 3.6--6\,\% compound annual growth across the modelled chains.

\subsection{Policy Implications}
For national and county governments, the framework offers a reproducible, biologically grounded baseline of exactly the kind that quantitative livestock planning in Sub-Saharan Africa has historically lacked.% TODO: cite (otte2002, jahnke1982)
Its architecture maps directly onto the analytical needs of national and county planning, furnishing the value-chain--specific projections required to prioritise investment and set performance targets.

The capacity to run coherent forward scenarios across nine value chains supports rational investment sequencing and can be institutionalised within national statistical and livestock agencies to move sector planning from static census snapshots toward continuous, data-driven foresight.% TODO: cite (bosire2022, knbs2019)

\subsection{Societal Implications}
At the societal scale, the framework speaks to the demand-driven transformation of animal-source-food consumption that will accompany Kenya's population growth and urbanisation.% TODO: cite (delgado1999)
Reliable projections of milk, meat, egg, and honey supply against rising demand are central to food and nutrition security planning, allowing anticipatory action on emerging supply gaps rather than reactive crisis response. Because livestock underpins the livelihoods of several million pastoralists and agro-pastoralists---and constitutes the dominant source of income across the arid and semi-arid lands---the model's capacity to anticipate feed shortfalls and productivity shocks has direct implications for poverty reduction, resilience, and the protection of vulnerable rural households~\cite{behnke2011}.
